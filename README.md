### 🚀 Project Overview

### The Problem

* Students, startups, and communities have great ideas and problems, but they work in isolated silos. Without a central platform to share funding and resources, brilliant concepts die before ever being built.

### Our Solution

* NexusOpen is a central hub that connects community problems with industry funding and student talent. It safely pools existing, underused resources to quickly turn abstract ideas into real-world applications..
